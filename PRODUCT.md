# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Sitting U.S. Senators, gubernatorial candidates, and congressional incumbents evaluating a media consultant. They arrive via referral or word-of-mouth. Context: a 2-minute research visit, usually from a laptop in an office or campaign war room. Job: quickly assess whether Left Hook's work and track record justify a call. They are sophisticated — they have seen every political vendor site. Generic impresses no one.

## Product Purpose

Portfolio and credibility site for Left Hook Communications, a Democratic political consulting and media firm founded by Brandon Hall. The site must immediately communicate award-winning craft, high-stakes experience, and the confidence of a firm that has won 8 of 11 U.S. Senate races. It exists to generate inbound calls and close retainer conversations. Success = a prospect picks up the phone.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic. Authoritative. Earned.

## Anti-references

- Generic political vendor sites (templated, stock-photo heavy, badge-and-logo grids)
- AI-generated layouts (Inter font, purple gradients, cookie-cutter card grids, hero metrics)
- Over-designed agency portfolios that obscure the work behind visual noise
- Anything that reads "tech startup" — this is a practitioner's shop, not a SaaS product

## Design Principles

1. **The work is the headline.** Every layout decision should frame the video ads as the product. Typography and structure serve the work, never compete with it.
2. **Earned authority.** No self-congratulatory language or flashy claims. The press quotes, the race record, the client list — these do the talking.
3. **Campaign poster energy.** Oversized display type, high contrast, monochromatic restraint with a single blue accent. Reads like a poster, not a brochure.
4. **Cinematic at every scale.** The video grid, the modal, the hover states — every interaction should feel like a film production house, not a marketing agency.
5. **Nothing generic survives.** If a design choice could belong to any other site, cut it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Keyboard navigation for video modal (Escape, Tab). Reduced motion media query should disable scroll reveals and scan-line animations. No reliance on color alone for meaning.
